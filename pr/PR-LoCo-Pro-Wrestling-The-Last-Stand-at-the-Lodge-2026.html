The Last Stand at the Lodge brings the Opera House saga to Longmont’s historic Elks Lodge Ballroom

LONGMONT, CO (July 13, 2026): LoCo Pro Wrestling, Longmont’s home-grown professional wrestling promotion, announces “The Last Stand at the Lodge” at the historic Elks Lodge Ballroom on Sunday, August 30, 2026. Doors open at 5:00 PM and the bell rings at 6:00 PM.

For the first time, LoCo Pro Wrestling steps beyond the Opera House and into the Elks Lodge Ballroom at 306 Coffman Street, a Longmont gathering place for a century, for a night where the promotion’s defining rivalry reaches its breaking point.

Locked out of the Opera House, Starling makes his stand

At Vendetta at the Opera House, Commissioner Adam Starling moved to break The Pillars’ exclusive hold on the building, but JT Staten was one step ahead. Staten disbanded The Pillars himself, rebuilt them as The Foundation, and watched as a brand-new exclusivity clause sealed the Opera House doors before Starling could act.

With the Opera House locked away, Starling found the one room in Longmont he knows as well as anyone: the Elks Lodge Ballroom, built in 1925. But a room is all he has. Staten holds the title. Staten holds the power. Staten holds all the cards. One question hangs over August 30th: what could possibly make JT Staten put that title on the line?

The full card will be announced in the weeks ahead.

Event highlights

  • The next chapter: A direct continuation of the Battle and Vendetta at the Opera House storyline.
  • Family-friendly entertainment: A live, in-person show suitable for all ages.
  • Historic venue: The Elks Lodge Ballroom offers an intimate, century-old setting for high-impact action.
  • One night only: Limited seating, with VIP front-row and second-row reserved options.

Quote

“The Opera House chapters were about who controls the stage. The Last Stand is about what happens when the stage itself is taken away,” said Aaron Macomber, owner of LoCo Pro Wrestling. “Starling has one room and one chance. On August 30th, Longmont finds out what a last stand really looks like.”

About LoCo Pro Wrestling

LoCo Pro Wrestling is a cinematic, story-driven professional wrestling promotion founded and based in Longmont, Colorado. Born in the underground and raised in the Rockies, LoCo Pro Wrestling blends mythology and mayhem to create compelling narratives that resonate with the local community. With roots deeply planted in Longmont’s culture, LoCo Pro Wrestling aims to provide inclusive entertainment, elevate local talent, and give back to the community that shaped it.

About the Elks Lodge Ballroom

Built in 1925, with its grand ballroom added in the 1960s, the Elks Lodge at 306 Coffman Street has been a Longmont gathering place for a century. Its ballroom offers a historic, intimate setting that continues to host community events, celebrations, and, for one night in August, professional wrestling.
