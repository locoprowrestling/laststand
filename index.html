The Night in One Line

Zeak Gallent beat JT Staten one-on-one for the City of Lights Championship. Then Carter Cash flattened the Commissioner, and Nicky Hyde stood over the new champion holding his belt.

What Happened

Nine Years After the First Bell,
Longmont Got a New Champion.

On August 30, 2017, LoCo Pro Wrestling held its first show, Battle for Main Street, on the second floor of the Dickens Opera House. Nine years to the day, the promotion filled the ballroom of Longmont Elks Lodge #1055, a block west on Coffman Street, for a night of buried history, new arrivals, broken alliances, and one enormous championship victory.

Commissioner Adam Starling opened the evening by saying the war between The Rising and The Pillars was never the whole story. There was a third voice, a rebellion redacted from Longmont's history, and it walked through the curtain in the form of "Box State Brawler" Murdoc Graves, XJ Khaos, Rubi Devlin, and Morgan Mandella.

In the main event, Zeak Gallent defeated JT Staten one-on-one to become the new City of Lights Champion. Nobody from The Foundation came to save Staten. What came instead was his past: a masked Vigilante who called him out mid-match, and a Commissioner who finally said out loud how much of the last nine months had been by design.

Official Results

The Card, in Order

Six matches, one opening segment, and one final image. Results as posted by LoCo Pro Wrestling after the show.

Opening Segment

A Third Voice Enters the Room

Debut Murdoc Graves XJ Khaos Truth Through Violence

Adam Starling opened the evening by saying the fight between The Rising and The Pillars was never the whole story. There was another voice: a rebellion redacted from Longmont's history. On this night it belonged to "Box State Brawler" Murdoc Graves.

"If you only have two choices, then you don't really have any choice at all." Murdoc Graves

Graves arrived alongside XJ Khaos, Rubi Devlin, and Morgan Mandella. They were not there to join somebody else's fight. They were there to start their own.

Match 1 · Tag Team

Rubi Devlin & Morgan Mandella def. Erza Menagerie Tinker & Anuka Gutierrez

Winners: Truth Through Violence Debut match

Rubi and Morgan called out Erza and Anuka the moment their introductions ended, then beat them in a hard-fought opener. Afterward, Erza and Anuka embraced and left LoCo Pro together. Their happy ending, for now.

They Were Forced to Fight: Erza and Anuka's journey (2:28)

Match 2 · Grudge Tag

Nicky Hyde & Carter Cash def. Fly Boi CJ Quinn & Mr. Wright

Winners: Hyde & Cash Substitution

Johnny Crash arrived ready to compete, but LoCo Pro's medical team could not clear him in time for the grudge match. Crash recruited the up-and-comer CJ Quinn to fight alongside Mr. Wright. Quinn competed with tremendous heart, but he and Wright came up short. Carter Cash and Nicky Hyde were clearly not on the same page, and they still found a way to win.

Match 3 · Triple Threat · Number-One Contender

Zeak Gallent def. "All Business" Dean Mercer and Codah Alexander

Winner: Zeak Gallent Title shot earned

Mercer brought the power. Codah brought the speed. Zeak survived both, won the triple threat, and earned a City of Lights Championship match later the same night. JT Staten was still waiting for him.

Intermission

Match 4 · Tag Team

Michael Avalon & Franky Gonzales def. Murdoc Graves & XJ Khaos

Winners: Pretty Great The handshake

Graves and Khaos came out first, ready to represent the rebellion. Michael Avalon came out to fight. Franky Gonzales was announced as his partner, but he appeared in street clothes, exchanged a few words with Avalon, and stepped away to watch from ringside. Avalon took the fight to both men by himself until the numbers began to overwhelm him.

Franky could only watch for so long. He hit the ring, the chemistry was immediate, and Pretty Great was back. They won with the Pretty Great finisher. Afterward, Avalon offered Franky his hand. This time, Franky accepted it.

Match 5 · Singles

Morgana Lavey def. Major Glory

Winner: Morgana Lavey, by pinfall Unexplained

Major Glory came through the curtain confused, agitated, and nothing like the Major Glory Longmont is used to seeing. Before the match could begin, cameraman Logan Chase caught his attention. Glory went after him, beat him down, dragged the attack into the ring, and slammed him before finally turning to Morgana Lavey.

Morgana appears to have some kind of control over Glory, though there were moments when he seemed to fight her influence, as if part of him knew something was wrong. Morgana pinned him. Then Glory did her bidding, lifted Morgana onto his shoulder, and carried her away. Why he attacked Logan, what Morgana is doing to him, and how much control he still has are open questions. Even Commissioner Adam Starling does not have those answers.

Main Event · City of Lights Championship

Zeak Gallent def. JT Staten (c)

New City of Lights Champion One-on-one The Vigilante appears

JT Staten entered expecting The Foundation to be there when he needed them. Nobody came. Nobody saved him. But his past did come back to find him. The Vigilante appeared and called JT out, pulling his attention from the match long enough for Zeak to try to capitalize. Staten survived the distraction, but the momentum had shifted. Zeak kept fighting, and when it was finally over he had beaten JT Staten one-on-one to become the new City of Lights Champion.

After the Bell

"Sorry, Zeak. I Lied to You."

Adam Starling Carter Cash Nicky Hyde

Commissioner Adam Starling stepped into the ring and raised Zeak's hand. Then he turned to JT Staten and said out loud how much of what Longmont had watched since November had been by design: every contract, every wrestler who stepped into a LoCo Pro ring, every dollar he spent, every decision that pushed The Rising and The Pillars toward this moment. His original plan had been to lock Staten out of the Opera House himself. Instead, Staten could keep it. LoCo Pro had found a new home in a beautiful building in the heart of Longmont, and Starling liked it that way.

Everything had been building toward the picture in front of him: Staten on the ground, The Rising victorious in the middle of the ring, and the Longmont faithful there to witness it. The wrestlers themselves, he admitted, had been part of his plan whether they knew it or not. Then he turned back to the man whose hand he had just raised.

"Sorry, Zeak. I lied to you." Commissioner Adam Starling

That is when Carter Cash's music hit. Carter stormed the ring and confronted the new champion, then turned toward the Commissioner and flattened him with a superkick. Before Zeak could process what had happened, Nicky Hyde attacked him from behind, picked up the City of Lights Championship, and stood over the fallen champion, staring Carter down with Zeak's title held above his head.

Carter Cash on one side. Nicky Hyde on the other. The new City of Lights Champion caught directly between them. That was the final image of Last Stand at the Lodge.

Where Things Stand

What Last Stand Changed

City of Lights Championship

Zeak Gallent

He won it one-on-one, with the Longmont faithful watching, and no paperwork can cover that. The last hands on the belt that night, however, were Nicky Hyde's.

The Challengers

Hyde and Cash

Nicky Hyde attacked the champion from behind and raised his belt. Carter Cash stood on the other side. The two men who won together earlier in the night, without ever being on the same page, ended it staring at each other over the title.

The Foundation

Nobody came

JT Staten built The Foundation to hold the building and the belt. When he needed it, no one answered. He lost the championship clean, and his faction's two most dangerous members finished the night squaring off with each other.

The Commissioner

By design

Adam Starling admitted that the past nine months were his plan and that the wrestlers were part of it whether they knew it or not. He apologized to Zeak for lying to him. Moments later, Carter Cash superkicked him. LoCo Pro has a new home. Its locker room just heard all of that.

The Rebellion

A third voice

Murdoc Graves, XJ Khaos, and Truth Through Violence (Rubi Devlin and Morgan Mandella) announced themselves as a third side in Longmont's fight. Their debut split: Rubi and Morgan won the opener, and Graves and Khaos fell to Pretty Great.

Loose Ends

Open questions

Erza Menagerie Tinker and Anuka Gutierrez left together, on their own terms. Morgana Lavey left on Major Glory's shoulder, and nobody can explain what she has done to him. Johnny Crash never got his grudge match. The Vigilante, the masked figure from LoCo Pro's first night in 2017, appeared again, and his business with JT Staten is not finished. Who is that mysterious masked man? Nobody in the building said.
